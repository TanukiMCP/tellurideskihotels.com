### Beginner Runs (Green Circles)

**1. Meadows Run**

Located in the Meadows Learning Area, this dedicated beginner terrain sits isolated from faster traffic, creating a safe environment perfect for first-time skiers. Magic carpet access eliminates chairlift challenges that can intimidate beginners, while gentle slopes allow skill development without pressure. The area's design creates safe learning environments where new skiers can build confidence at their own pace.

**2. Galloping Goose**

This long, gentle run stretches through lower Mountain Village, providing the perfect progression run after mastering basics in the Meadows area. The wide trail allows comfortable skiing without feeling crowded, while the consistent gentle pitch provides learning opportunities without overwhelming challenges. Many skiers use Galloping Goose as their first "real" run after graduating from the learning area.

**3. The Plunge (Lower Section)**

The lower section of Telluride's iconic Plunge run offers accessible beginner terrain with views of the surrounding San Juan Mountains. This section provides safe beginner terrain while the upper sections offer natural progression opportunities as skills develop. The Plunge's lower section serves as an excellent introduction to Telluride's terrain, giving beginners a taste of the mountain's character without overwhelming difficulty.
